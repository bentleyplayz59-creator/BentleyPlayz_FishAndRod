BentleyPlayz Fish & Rod v1.1.0
Requires: SMAPI 4.x, Content Patcher, Stardew Valley 1.6+
Install: unzip into Stardew Valley/Mods (folder BentleyPlayz_FishAndRod, contains the DLL mod and the [CP] content pack).
Requires the DLL: BentleyPlayz.BentleyFishCore (included).

WHAT IT DOES
- 11 new fish, catchable in every season, every weather, all day, in every fishable water.
- Normal rods: about 10% of casts give one of the new fish.
- BentleyPlayz Rod (Willy's shop, 1,500g): about 90% of casts. Hold it while fishing.
- Windex Fish / Tide Pod Trout: eating raw hurts you.
- Cook the Windex Fish (plus any forage item) into the Tide-Pod-Free Windex Fillet.

TUNING (content.json -> Data/Locations -> Chance / RodBoost Amount)
- Chance is per fish roll. With 11 fish: 0.0105 -> ~10% total, 0.2057 -> ~90% total.

NEW IN 1.1.0
- Bentley's Golden Lure (Willy's shop, 500g): attach to any rod, every catch is one of our fish.
- The BentleyPlayz Rod is drawn in the farmer's hands while fishing (offsets adjustable in config or Generic Mod Config Menu).
